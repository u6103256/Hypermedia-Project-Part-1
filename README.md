# Hypermedia-Project-Part-1

## User Profile:
My website is designed with a specific target user in mind: a hiring manager looking to find the right candidate for the job.

This person is busy reviewing a large number of CVs and needs to choose from many applicants. Therefore, they appreciate a CV that is easy to read, gets straight to the point, and highlights the most important information.

## Information Architecture:
For this curriculum vitae, I divided the page into two sections: a vertical sidebar, where I included my picture, contact details and secondary information, such as the languages I speak and my skills; and a main section, where I presented the most important information, including a brief introduction about myself, my previous work experience, and my educational background.

The information is organized vertically, ordered by their importance in their respective sections, and it is clearly divided so it is easier for the user to find the information they're looking for.

In the main section, my name is placed prominently at the top of the page, making my identity immediately visible. Below it, the content follows a logical order, starting with a short professional introduction, followed by my work experience and academic background. This sequence allows the reader to understand my professional profile before exploring my qualifications in greater detail.

The sidebar has a light blue background that visually separates it from the main content area. In it we can find my picture on top, making it the first thing the user will see, and then it is followed by my contact data, my skills and thereafter the languages I speak.

## Visual Design:
The style chosen for this CV is clean, professional, and minimalist. The main objective is to create a modern and well-organized design that communicates professionalism while making the information easy to read and understand.

The light blue sidebar creates a clear distinction between the two main sections, while the darker blue headings help highlight important information and maintain visual consistency throughout the page. The white background of the main section also provides contrast and allows the text to stand out.

The typography is simple and readable, ensuring that the content can be scanned quickly. Bold text is used to emphasize key information, such as job titles and academic qualifications, helping the reader identify the most relevant details at a glance. The consistent use of headings, spacing, and alignment contributes to a balanced and structured layout.
